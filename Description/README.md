🟡 PAC-SHOWDOWN 3D

⚡ THE ULTIMATE PAC-MAN MULTIPLAYER SHOWDOWN ⚡

Pac-Showdown is a 3D multiplayer Battle Royale inspired by classic Pac-Man action, but taken to a competitive arena where up to 10 players fight to get the highest score and survive.

🏆 Will you be the last player standing or get the highest score?

---

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

Each game starts with 2 to 10 players inside a huge 3D maze.

Your objective is simple:

- 🟡 Eat dots and Power Pellets.

- ⚡ Get special powers.

- 👻 Survive the ghosts.

- 🏃 Move quickly through the maze.

- 🏆 Get the highest score possible.

- 👑 Try to be the last player standing!

Points and Power Pellets regenerate continuously, so the action never stops.

---

⏱️ 5 MINUTES OF CHAOS

Each Showdown has a 5-minute time limit.

But when only 1 minute remains...

🚨 GLITCH 256 INVASION 🚨

The maze begins to close in from the edges, progressively reducing the available space and forcing players to fight in an increasingly smaller area.

The music changes, and the Sudden Death phase begins.

---

👻 THE GHOSTS

The maze is inhabited by 8 different ghosts, each with their own unique behavior:

- 🔴 BLINKY — Chase
- 🩷 PINKY — Ambush
- 🔵 INKY — Flank
- 🟠 CLYDE — Wander
- 🟣 TIM — Chase
- 🟢 SPIKE — Ambush
- 🟡 GOLDY — Wander
- ⚪ WHITY — Flank

Not all of them chase you the same way!

---

⚡ POWER-UPS

During the game, you can acquire different power-ups that change your strategy:

- 🟡 POWER PELLET
- 🟠 LASER
- 🟢 SPEED BOOST
- 🩵 GHOST FREEZE

Using them at the right moment can completely change the outcome of the Showdown!

---

🌐 MULTIPLAYER

Pac-Showdown lets you create online games using rooms.

🏠 CREATE GAME

Create a new room and share the code.

🚪 JOIN GAME

Enter the code of an existing room to join.

Games support up to 10 simultaneous players.

---

🏆 TROPHY CABINET

After each game, players receive podium positions:

🥇 1ST — GOLD
🥈 2ND — SILVER
🥉 3RD — BRONZE

Your trophies are saved locally so you can try to increase your collection with each game.

---

🎮 CONTROLS

- 🕹️ Virtual Joystick — movement
- 👆 Drag the screen — control the camera
- 🔼 Jump — jump

💻 PC

- W / A / S / D — movement
- Arrow keys — movement
- SPACE — jump
